Privacy Policy — Hogo Plus FS

Effective date: 18 September 2026 · Applies to the Hogo Plus FS mobile application and the associated management dashboard operated by Prasad Sugar & Allied Agro Products Ltd (“we”, “us”, the “Company”).

1. What this app is

Hogo Plus FS is an internal workforce application used by employees and management of the Company's factory in Maharashtra, India, for attendance recording, incident/complaint reporting, shift management and factory operations. It is intended solely for the Company's employees and authorised personnel; accounts are provisioned and managed by the Company's Time Office.

3. Live location during your shift (pilot programme)

  • Only during your shift: tracking can start only after you punch in and stops automatically at punch-out (and in any case ~30 minutes after your shift ends). Outside your shift the app never records or sends your location.
  • Only with your consent: tracking never starts unless you have accepted the in-app consent screen (available in Marathi, Hindi and English). You can decline; declining never blocks attendance or any other feature.
  • What is recorded: the name of the factory zone whose Bluetooth beacon your phone hears, or your GPS position when no beacon is nearby, with battery level. No microphone, camera or movement data is involved.
  • Who can see it: only the Company's top management (MD/CGM), the Time Office, Security, and your own department manager — each view is recorded in the audit trail.
  • Automatic deletion: the location trail is purged automatically after 30 days. The live snapshot is overwritten continuously.
  • Honesty by design: if you stop tracking or switch off Bluetooth/GPS, the dashboard shows “tracking stopped / no signal” — the app never fabricates a location.

4. Face verification (biometric processing)

When you punch in, the selfie you take is compared with your stored reference selfie using Amazon Web Services (AWS) Rekognition, which returns a similarity score. Images are transmitted securely for this comparison and are not used by us or AWS to build unrelated face databases or for advertising. If verification cannot be completed, your punch is placed in a supervised review queue for the Time Office instead — no automatic adverse action is taken against you by the software alone. Your first approved selfie becomes your reference; the Time Office can reset it at any time on request.

5. Processors and where data is stored

  • AWS Rekognition (Amazon Web Services) — face comparison and image-text detection, processed on our behalf.
  • Cloud object storage (Cloudflare R2 / S3-compatible) — encrypted storage of photos, videos, voice notes and nightly reports.
  • Neon (PostgreSQL cloud database, Asia-Pacific region) — application records.
  • SMSGatewayHub — delivery of one-time login passwords to your phone number.
  • Expo push notification service — delivery of work notifications to your device.

These providers act as processors under our instructions and do not sell your data or use it for their own marketing. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

6. Retention

  • Attendance records, incident reports and media: retained for the duration of your employment and thereafter as required for the Company's statutory, payroll and audit obligations.
  • Live-location trail (shift tracking): purged automatically after 30 days.
  • Reference selfie and face-comparison scores: retained while your account is active; deleted or reset when your employment ends or upon approved request to the Time Office.
  • OTP and login logs: retained for a short operational period for security and troubleshooting, then purged.
  • Backups: encrypted backups are kept on a rolling schedule and expire automatically.

7. Your rights and account deletion

Subject to applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and may raise grievances about its processing.

Delete your account in the app: open Profile → Delete my account, confirm the warning and verify with the one-time password sent to your phone. This immediately and permanently deletes your name, phone number, selfies, face-verification data, push token and the live-location trail, and signs you out everywhere. Attendance, incident and audit records that the Company must keep by law are retained in anonymised form (“Deleted user #ID”). You may also request deletion in person at the Time Office or by email to the contact below.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to records is role-restricted (e.g., attendance review is limited to the Time Office and top management), and administrative actions are recorded in an audit trail.

9. Children

The app is intended for adult employees only and is not directed at children.

10. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera — attendance selfies and incident photos/videos.
  • Microphone — voice notes attached to incident reports.
  • Location (precise, while in use) — presence verification at punch, incident location tagging, and consent-based shift tracking. The app does not request background location.
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth — detecting Company-installed attendance beacons inside the factory.
  • Notifications — work alerts, approvals and the visible “shift tracking on” notice.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified in the app and the revised policy will be published at this address.

१. हे अ‍ॅप काय आहे

Hogo Plus FS हे कंपनीच्या महाराष्ट्रातील कारखान्यातील कर्मचारी व व्यवस्थापनासाठीचे अंतर्गत अ‍ॅप आहे — हजेरी, तक्रार नोंद, शिफ्ट व्यवस्थापन आणि कारखाना कामकाजासाठी. खाती टाइम ऑफिसकडून दिली जातात.

२. आम्ही कोणती माहिती घेतो

  • ओळख व खाते: पूर्ण नाव, कर्मचारी ID, विभाग, भूमिका, फोन नंबर, भाषा.
  • हजेरी सेल्फी व चेहरा: पंच-इन सेल्फी, संदर्भ सेल्फी, चेहरा-जुळणी स्कोअर (हजेरी खरी आहे हे तपासण्यासाठी).
  • लोकेशन: पंच व तक्रारीच्या वेळचे GPS; कारखान्यातील ब्लूटूथ बीकनचा झोन.
  • शिफ्टमधील लाइव्ह लोकेशन (पायलट, संमतीनेच): बीकन झोनचे नाव किंवा बीकन नसल्यास GPS, बॅटरी — फक्त पंच-इन असताना.
  • तक्रार मीडिया: फोटो, व्हिडिओ, आवाज नोंदी.
  • डिव्हाइस: नोटिफिकेशन टोकन, अ‍ॅप भाषा व आवृत्ती.

आम्ही तुमचे संपर्क, मेसेज, आर्थिक माहिती घेत नाही; अ‍ॅपमध्ये जाहिराती नाहीत.

३. शिफ्टमधील लाइव्ह लोकेशन

  • फक्त शिफ्टमध्ये: पंच-इन नंतरच सुरू; पंच-आउट झाल्यावर लगेच बंद (शिफ्ट संपल्यावर ~३० मिनिटांत आपोआप बंद). शिफ्टबाहेर लोकेशन कधीही घेतले जात नाही.
  • फक्त तुमच्या संमतीने: अ‍ॅपमधील संमती स्क्रीन (मराठी/हिंदी/इंग्रजी) मान्य केल्याशिवाय ट्रॅकिंग सुरू होत नाही. नकार दिल्यास हजेरी वा इतर कोणतीही सुविधा थांबत नाही.
  • काय नोंदते: झोनचे नाव किंवा GPS व बॅटरी. मायक्रोफोन/कॅमेरा नाही.
  • कोण बघू शकते: फक्त MD/CGM, टाइम ऑफिस, सुरक्षा विभाग आणि तुमच्या विभागाचे व्यवस्थापक — प्रत्येक पाहणीची नोंद होते.
  • आपोआप डिलीट: लोकेशनची नोंद ३० दिवसांनी आपोआप डिलीट होते.
  • प्रामाणिक नोंद: ट्रॅकिंग बंद केल्यास डॅशबोर्डवर “ट्रॅकिंग बंद / सिग्नल नाही” असेच दिसते.
