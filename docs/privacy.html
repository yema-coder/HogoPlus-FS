Privacy Policy — Hogo Plus FS

Effective date: 28 July 2026 · Applies to the Hogo Plus FS mobile application and the associated management dashboard operated by Prasad Sugar & Allied Agro Products Ltd (“we”, “us”, the “Company”).

1. What this app is

Hogo Plus FS is an internal workforce application used by employees and management of the Company's factory in Maharashtra, India, for attendance recording, incident/complaint reporting, shift management and factory operations. It is intended solely for the Company's employees and authorised personnel; accounts are provisioned and managed by the Company's Time Office.

3. Face verification (biometric processing)

When you punch in, the selfie you take is compared with your stored reference selfie using Amazon Web Services (AWS) Rekognition, which returns a similarity score. Images are transmitted securely for this comparison and are not used by us or AWS to build unrelated face databases or for advertising. If verification cannot be completed, your punch is placed in a supervised review queue for the Time Office instead — no automatic adverse action is taken against you by the software alone. Your first approved selfie becomes your reference; the Time Office can reset it at any time on request.

4. Processors and where data is stored

  • AWS Rekognition (Amazon Web Services) — face comparison and image-text detection, processed on our behalf.
  • Cloud object storage (Cloudflare R2 / S3-compatible) — encrypted storage of photos, videos, voice notes and nightly reports.
  • Neon (PostgreSQL cloud database, Asia-Pacific region) — application records.
  • SMSGatewayHub — delivery of one-time login passwords to your phone number.
  • Expo push notification service — delivery of work notifications to your device.

These providers act as processors under our instructions and do not sell your data or use it for their own marketing. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

5. Retention

  • Attendance records, incident reports and media: retained for the duration of your employment and thereafter as required for the Company's statutory, payroll and audit obligations.
  • Reference selfie and face-comparison scores: retained while your account is active; deleted or reset when your employment ends or upon approved request to the Time Office.
  • OTP and login logs: retained for a short operational period for security and troubleshooting, then purged.
  • Backups: encrypted backups are kept on a rolling schedule and expire automatically.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and may raise grievances about its processing. Requests can be made in person at the Time Office or by email to the contact below. Account deletion: because accounts are employer-provisioned, deletion requests are actioned by the Time Office; your data will be removed except where retention is legally required.

7. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to records is role-restricted (e.g., attendance review is limited to the Time Office and top management), and administrative actions are recorded in an audit trail.

8. Children

The app is intended for adult employees only and is not directed at children.

9. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera — attendance selfies and incident photos/videos.
  • Microphone — voice notes attached to incident reports.
  • Location (precise) — presence verification at punch and incident location tagging.
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth — detecting Company-installed attendance beacons inside the factory.
  • Notifications — work alerts and approvals.
