Privacy Policy

HogoPlus-FS — Factory Safety & Attendance (Android app, package com.hogoplus.fs)
Effective date: 28 July 2026 · Operated by the HogoPlus team ("we", "us")

HogoPlus-FS is a workplace safety, incident-reporting and attendance application deployed for factory organisations ("your employer"). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and the rights you have. The app is used in an employment context: your employer is the primary controller of workplace records created in the app, and we operate the platform on their behalf.

1. Data we collect

  • Identity & contact: your name, mobile phone number, employee ID, department, role and shift, as registered by your employer or during sign-up.
  • Authentication: one-time passwords (OTPs) sent to your phone number by SMS to log you in. We keep a log of OTP send attempts for security and troubleshooting.
  • Attendance verification: a selfie photograph taken at each attendance punch. Your first supervised selfie is stored as a reference image, and later punch selfies are compared against it to confirm identity (see Section 3).
  • Location: your device's precise GPS coordinates at the moment of an attendance punch or incident report, used to confirm presence on factory premises; and nearby Bluetooth beacon zone identifiers installed by your employer (e.g. "Main Gate"), used to record the work zone. The app does not track your location in the background.
  • Incident content: photos, videos, voice recordings and text you submit when reporting a safety incident, including any vehicle number plates detected in photos you take.
  • Device & technical data: push-notification token, device model, app version, language preference, and audit logs of actions taken in the app (for accountability of approvals and administrative changes).

2. How we use this data

  • To log you in securely and operate your account.
  • To record and verify attendance (selfie + location + optional beacon zone).
  • To route, manage and resolve safety incident reports within your organisation.
  • To notify you of approvals, escalations and announcements.
  • To generate workplace safety reports and dashboards for authorised factory management.
  • To secure the platform, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot problems.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

3. Face data (important)

At each attendance punch, the selfie you take is compared with your stored reference selfie using Amazon Rekognition (AWS, Mumbai region) to produce a similarity score. Images are stored as ordinary photographs in our cloud storage; Rekognition processes them to perform the comparison and does not retain them for its own purposes.

  • Face comparison is used only to verify attendance identity — for no other purpose.
  • Uncertain or low-score punches are reviewed by an authorised human (Time Office / management) before any decision; the system does not take adverse action on its own.
  • Your reference selfie can be reset by the Time Office, after which your next supervised punch records a new one.

4. Who can see your data

  • Your employer: authorised managers, the Time Office and factory leadership can see attendance records, incident reports and related workplace data, as part of normal workplace administration.
  • Service providers (processors) that run the platform under our instructions:
    • Amazon Web Services — Rekognition (face comparison, image text detection), Mumbai region
    • Cloudflare R2 — encrypted cloud storage for photos, videos, voice notes and backups
    • Neon — managed PostgreSQL database
    • Upstash — managed Redis (sessions, rate-limiting)
    • SMSGatewayHub — delivery of OTP SMS messages
    • Expo — push-notification delivery

We do not share your personal data with any other third party except where required by law.

5. Retention

Workplace records (attendance, incidents, forms, audit logs) are retained for the duration of the deployment and as required by your employer's record-keeping obligations. Reference selfies are kept while you remain an active user. Encrypted database backups are kept on a rolling schedule and pruned automatically. When data is deleted, it is also removed from live systems; residual copies in backups expire with the backup cycle.

6. Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to records is role-based — workers, managers, the Time Office and leadership each see only what their role permits — and administrative actions are recorded in an audit log.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data, and you may raise a grievance about how it is handled. Requests relating to workplace records may be routed through your employer as the data fiduciary for those records. To exercise any right, contact us at the address below; we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

HogoPlus-FS is a workplace application intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect children's data.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
